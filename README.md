# MessLink

**Connecting students with mess & tiffin owners — with verified reviews and organized management, in one app.**

---

## Problem

Students have no reliable way to judge a mess or tiffin service's food quality or hygiene before subscribing — most rely on word-of-mouth. On the other side, mess and tiffin owners are small, non-technical operators who manage their business manually through phone calls, WhatsApp groups, and paper registers, with no organized system for tracking students, subscriptions, or payments.

## Solution

MessLink is a two-sided marketplace app that brings students and mess/tiffin owners onto one platform — giving students trustworthy discovery and reviews, and giving owners a simple digital system to replace manual tracking.

## Key Features

### For Students
- Search and filter mess/tiffin listings by cuisine, price, and meal type
- View mess profiles with photos and pricing plans before subscribing
- Verified reviews — open only to students with an active or past subscription, so ratings can't be faked
- Sub-ratings for taste, hygiene, quantity, and punctuality (not just one star average)

### For Mess/Tiffin Owners
- Dashboard to manage the student roster and subscription status
- Daily headcount tracking for meal planning
- Broadcast messaging for menu changes, holidays, or updates
- Built-in complaint/feedback resolution tracker

### Payments
- Recurring subscription billing via UPI
- Renewal reminders before each billing cycle
- Owner payout dashboard with revenue and pending dues
- Auto-generated invoices
